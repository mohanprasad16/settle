How it works

  1. Bring three files

    Razorpay settlement report, your bank statement (CSV or PDF, even scanned or locked) and your orders export. Or connect Razorpay directly.

  2. Code matches every rupee

    Fees, GST, refunds, chargebacks and timing are explained line by line until nothing is left unexplained. The AI only writes the explanations, and every figure it writes is checked.

  3. Approve the fixes

    Tickets to Razorpay, notes to customers and journal entries are drafted for you. Nothing is sent until a named person approves it. Download the audit report.

Working…

Connect Razorpay

In live mode you enter a read-only Razorpay API key. Settle pulls your settlement report every morning, matches it to your bank and books, and alerts you only when something new is wrong, so a missing settlement is seen the same day, not at month end.

This demo runs on sample data.Key entry is switched off here. The connector uses Razorpay's settlement recon API, the same columns as the file upload.
