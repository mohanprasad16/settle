Settle

Find the money that went missing between Razorpay and your bank.

Give Settle the three files you already have. It matches every payment, refund, fee and settlement, shows what went missing and why, and drafts the fixes for you to approve.

The sample is a fictional coffee brand's October. Nothing to sign up for, nothing is sent anywhere.

What you need: three files you already have

  1. ₹

    Razorpay settlement report

    Razorpay Dashboard, Reports, Settlement reconciliation, download as CSV. It lists every payment, refund and fee in each payout.

  2. ⌂

    Your bank statement

    The CSV from net banking, or the PDF your bank emails you. Scanned and password-locked PDFs work; Settle reads them and checks every balance.

  3. ≡

    Your orders

    An export from your store or accounts with order number, date, amount, status and refunds. Shopify, WooCommerce or Tally exports are fine.

Connect Razorpay

In live mode you add a read-only Razorpay API key. Every morning Settle pulls your settlement report, matches it to your bank and books, and tells you only when something new is wrong, so a missing settlement is seen the same day instead of at month end.

This demo runs on sample data. Key entry is switched off here. The connector uses Razorpay's settlement reconciliation API, the same columns as the file upload.
