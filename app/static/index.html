Settle

Find the money that went missing between your books, payments and bank.

Settle ties your orders, your payment gateway (Razorpay today) and your bank statement to the paisa. It matches every payment, refund, fee and settlement, shows what went missing and why, and drafts the fixes for you to approve. Upload three files, or connect the sources once.

The sample is a fictional coffee brand's October. Nothing to sign up for, nothing is sent anywhere.

What you need: three files you already have

  1. ₹

    Payment gateway settlements

    From Razorpay today: Dashboard, Reports, Settlement reconciliation, download as CSV. It lists every payment, refund and fee in each payout.

  2. ⌂

    Your bank statement

    The CSV from net banking, or the PDF your bank emails you. Scanned and password-locked PDFs work; Settle reads them and checks every balance.

  3. ≡

    Your orders

    An export from your store or accounts with order number, date, amount, status and refunds. Shopify, WooCommerce or Tally exports are fine.

Settle

Connect your sources

Settle reconciles three sources: your payment gateway, your bank and your books. Connect them once and it pulls all three every morning, reconciles, and tells you only about new problems.

This is a demo, so it runs on sample data. Live mode connects with a read-only key or your consent; those fields are switched off here. "Run a demo sync" loads the sample month as if it came from all three sources.
₹

Payment gateway

Razorpay

Demo: sample data

Pulls: every settlement and the payments, refunds, chargebacks and fees inside it.

Connects with: Razorpay's Settlement Reconciliation API and a read-only API key. The connector is built; uploads use the same columns.

More gateways (Cashfree, PayU, Stripe) would plug in the same way. Not built yet.

Last synced: never

⌂

Bank

Your current account

Demo: sample data

Pulls: the statement lines, with the UTR or reference on each credit.

Connects with: the Account Aggregator framework (RBI's consent-based way to share bank data), your bank's API, or a RazorpayX current account. Or upload the CSV or PDF statement, even scanned or password-locked.

Last synced: never
